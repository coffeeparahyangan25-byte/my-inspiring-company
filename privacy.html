Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PT Cipta Inspirasi Sejahtera, operating MY INSPIRING and MY INSPIRING Affiliate Intelligence, handles information used in our internal digital commerce and affiliate operations.

When an authorized account connects to our application, we may process information made available through approved platform APIs, including authorized account information, affiliate collaboration information, product and showcase information, and related operational or performance data, subject to the permissions granted.

2. How we use information

Information may be used to operate authorized affiliate workflows, conduct product intelligence and research, manage permitted showcase or collaboration workflows, analyze operational performance, maintain application functionality, and comply with applicable platform requirements.

3. Authorization and access

We access third-party platform information only through official authorization mechanisms and approved API permissions. Authorization may be revoked or restricted in accordance with the procedures provided by the relevant platform.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. Information may be processed by service providers where reasonably necessary to operate our systems, or disclosed where required by law, regulation, or applicable platform requirements.

5. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect credentials and information used by our systems. Application secrets, access tokens, refresh tokens, and similar credentials are not intended to be publicly disclosed.

6. Data retention and deletion

We retain information only as reasonably necessary for the operational purposes described above, applicable legal obligations, and platform requirements. Authorized users may request deletion of information associated with our application, subject to applicable legal and platform retention requirements.

7. Third-party platforms

Our systems may integrate with third-party commerce or social platforms. Their processing of information is governed by their own terms and privacy policies. References to third-party platforms do not imply endorsement or official partnership unless expressly stated.

8. Contact

For privacy or data-related requests concerning MY INSPIRING Affiliate Intelligence, please contact PT Cipta Inspirasi Sejahtera through the business contact information supplied in the relevant authorization or partner registration process.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as our systems, integrations, or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page.
