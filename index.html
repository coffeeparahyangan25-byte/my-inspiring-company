About the company

PT Cipta Inspirasi Sejahtera is an Indonesian company engaged in commercial digital platform activities and general trading. Through MY INSPIRING, the company develops and operates internal technology and workflows supporting e-commerce product research, product intelligence, affiliate commerce, and digital content operations.

MY INSPIRING Affiliate Intelligence

MY INSPIRING Affiliate Intelligence is an internal application intended to improve the efficiency and accuracy of our affiliate operations. Its intended functions include authorized creator affiliate information, collaboration product research, showcase product management, workflow automation, and related operational performance analysis.

The application is intended for our own business operations and authorized accounts rather than as a public consumer application or an agency service for third-party clients.
